# Snail32 — Example Questions

Six questions an engineer would usually ask about the ESP32-S3, transplanted to the Snail32.

1. What are Snail32's CPU cores, clock speed, and memory — SRAM, flash, and PSRAM options — and how do they compare with the ESP32-S3?
2. Which Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions does Snail32 support, and what are its RF figures — TX power and receive sensitivity — against the ESP32-S3?
3. How many GPIOs and which peripherals does Snail32 have — ADC, touch, USB OTG, LCD and camera interfaces — and is the pinout a drop-in match for the ESP32-S3?
4. What does Snail32 draw in active, modem-sleep, and deep-sleep modes, and what supply voltage does it need?
5. Can I build Snail32 firmware with ESP-IDF, and how much of an existing ESP32-S3 codebase ports over unchanged?
6. What are Snail32's unit price, MOQ, and lead time, and what is its certification status — FCC, CE, RoHS?
