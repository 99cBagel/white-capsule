## What the buyer must still do

Until Snail32 holds its own modular certification, a buyer integrating it
must do their own host-device certification (FCC / CE) — or wait. The
evaluation must say this plainly, with the cost and time that adds. Never
imply "FCC certified" from a test report that is not a grant.

## Rules

- A claim of certification without a certificate number is a rumor.
- A test report is not a certificate.
- `planned` needs a target date or it is `unknown`.
